Summary

Turkish Nexa community awaits the Tailstorm hard fork, cheers a price uptick, and debates macro and Trump

Over roughly a week, the Turkish Nexa group centred on the upcoming Tailstorm protocol upgrade and the hard fork it requires. Moderator Onur_Chinaski confirmed the hard fork is coming but said no date can be given, while X relayed that Andrew Stone considers Blitz and the Tokadex DEX essentially finished pending a hard-fork feature; Blacktea posted the official call to update nodes to Full-Node 2.2.0.0 (rolling checkpoints, last release before the fork). Price talk was frequent and repeatedly redirected by the Rose bot to the unofficial trade channel: a stubborn 14B sell wall, hopes of breaking '130', and a Monday pump that Green Zenci and Hakan greeted enthusiastically. Several members (X in particular) pushed the group to be more active on X/Twitter, complaining that only 30 of 631 members are online. The window closes with a macro/politics debate between Onur_Chinaski and X about US rate hikes, Houthi attacks on Saudi refineries pushing Brent to $109, and whether Trump staying or going would help or delay a bull run. Minor housekeeping included CAS bans, scam accounts being removed, and help joining the trade channel.
