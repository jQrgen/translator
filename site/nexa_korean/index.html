Summary

A slow-dying Korean Nexa group: price despair, mining-vs-buying advice, wallet headaches, and two flare-ups

This window spans roughly two and a half years (Nov 2023 – Apr 2026) in a very low-traffic Korean group, so conversations are short bursts separated by weeks or months. Early on 바 맨 and PLUS W chat about whether Nexa has bottomed, compare other mining coins (Qubic, Firo) and decide to just hold into spring, while 바 맨 says he's done with mining coins entirely. The final messages are link drops, a 2026 market-status post, and Russian spam.
