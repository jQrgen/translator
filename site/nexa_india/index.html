Summary

Six months of Nexa announcements: first halving, Tailstorm hard-fork prep, NexaPoW 2.0, plus grassroots India adoption talk

This window is dominated by official announcements posted by Blacktea ▽ (later in Hindi), with relatively little back-and-forth discussion. The centrepiece is Nexa's first halving on 1 May 2026, covered by a countdown series, the Halving Fest art contest and a post-halving X Spaces, followed in June by the 4-year anniversary Spaces with founder Andrew Stone. Technically, the recurring thread is preparation for Hard Fork #2 and Tailstorm (DAG-hybrid) integration, alongside NexScript v1.1.0 and Playground upgrades, explorer improvements, LibNexaKotlin/Wally wallet releases, and from August a push on NexaPoW 2.0 and Peter Rizun's Blitz hardware/'node made of wires' article series, capped by Full-Node 2.2.0.0 as the last release before the fork. On the community side, DhammaDana posted long Hindi essays urging members to act as evangelists and describing real OTC/P2P NEXA trading at 10-20% local premiums in Indian and Nepali small towns and villages. Darker notes are rare but present: warnings that Nexamine is not an official project, and notices that Exbitron and BitMart are shutting down. A brief moderation episode on 1 September saw Blacktea enable antiraid while Asin argued against blocking genuine new joiners.
