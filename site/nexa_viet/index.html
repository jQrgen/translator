Summary

A near-dormant group: bot welcomes, occasional price gripes, one real mining chat and a moderation spat.

This window covers roughly two years (Nov 2024 – Sep 2026) of a very quiet channel, where the majority of messages are the Rose bot's automated welcome and departure lines plus scattered 'GM' greetings from Promanon "NTR, Onur_Chinaski (mod) and DhammaDana. Genuine conversation is rare: Royan asked about Nexa's market cap and Promanon pointed to CoinGecko (~$8.4M), while Huy8x, Khanh Nguyen84, R P and Nexa Nexa repeatedly questioned whether the group or the project is dead as the price kept sliding. Nexa Nexa also accused the team of 'only talking a good game' and complained about a ban, prompting Onur_Chinaski to explain that criticism is fine but personal attacks are not. The most substantive exchange came in August 2026, when Nexa Nexa and Quin Nguyen discussed mining economics — Quin mines ~18M NEXA/day (down from 20M+), runs at a loss on electricity but finds it better than INI, and is holding rather than selling, with Nexa Nexa mentioning an expected hardfork in about two months. Brighter notes include DhammaDana's report of meetings with officials in Sravasti about Nexa collaboration, Zhang Qian noting a sharp price rally in April 2026, and several Russian-language spam accounts drifting through.
