Summary

Community-built prediction site, a modest NEXA price rally, and node 2.2.0.0 / Tailstorm fork talk

Over roughly ten days the Russian Nexa group mixed light community projects with price and protocol discussion. Woldemar Kulachesko and Alex ▼ promoted a free, no-stakes Premier League prediction site that uses Wally wallet login, and Alex ▼ quickly added CS2 and Dota sections at High Voltage's suggestion. The bigger thread was price: after Max complained growth had stalled, Лил, Sergey Ionov, Bossmen and Woldemar Kulachesko debated whether a real rally had begun, whether whales/ASIC miners would dump again (Bossmen noting there are no buy walls below), and whether NEXA can reclaim old highs — with some jokes that such prices might take 40 years. Лил raised harder questions about whether only die-hard fans remain and whether the project will survive, while Bossmen argued the team at least hasn't rugged. Blacktea ▽ posted the official announcement for Full-Node 2.2.0.0 with rolling checkpoints (200-block finality), the last release before the Tailstorm hard fork, plus a Peter Rizun 'scaling by circuit' article. That triggered a technical exchange in which Woldemar Kulachesko and Alex ▼ explained GPUs stay, old ASICs become obsolete, and a Game-of-Life-based changing algorithm would make ASICs inefficient.
