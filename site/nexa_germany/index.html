    The Nexa team's core developers are integrating Tailstorm, a block-DAG-based design built on the Tailstorm research. The Tailstorm model represents a significant architectural advance: it moves away from a linear blockchain towards a directed acyclic graph (DAG) of subblocks while retaining the conventional chain of “summary blocks”. By merging the summary block model with the traditional chain, Nexa can leverage the advantages of both approaches while at the same time employing more advanced mining and block propagation mechanisms.
